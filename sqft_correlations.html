Key takeaways for investors:
• $/sqft in Heights has risen ~4.7× since 1997, with acceleration 2012–2022 and moderation thereafter.
• Strong positive long-term co-movement with Harris County median household income (income growth supports higher local purchasing power).
• Inverse relationship with mortgage rates is visible in rate-hike periods (2018, 2022–23); rate relief historically coincides with faster $/sqft gains.
• Population / net migration: Harris County continues to grow overall (international + natural increase offset domestic outflows). Local demand remains supported by metro growth even when domestic net migration is negative.
• Business establishments (Harris County) have expanded over the cycle, providing a positive backdrop for commercial/residential synergy in inner-loop neighborhoods like the Heights.
• Correlations are directional and lag-sensitive; local supply constraints (historic districts) amplify the impact of favorable macro conditions.
